WHO IS LUMI?

Meet the little light behind STEM Without Barriers. ✨

Lumi is the friendly mascot of STEM Without Barriers. She represents curiosity, creativity, and the idea that every student deserves the opportunity to explore STEM.

Whether she's experimenting with a new idea, building something from scratch, or asking "what if?", Lumi reminds students that there is no single way to be a STEM learner.

She's here to make STEM feel a little less intimidating, a lot more welcoming, and always worth exploring.

LUMI'S MISSION

Making STEM feel possible for everyone.

Lumi represents everything STEM Without Barriers stands for: curiosity, accessibility, creativity, and the confidence to keep asking questions.
