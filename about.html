ABOUT STEM WITHOUT BARRIERS

Making STEM Accessible For Every Learner

We believe every student deserves the opportunity to explore, create, discover, and see themselves in STEM.

OUR MISSION

STEM should have no barriers.

To make STEM education accessible, inclusive, and exciting for every child, regardless of background or ability.

Through free resources, hands-on activities, workshops, mentorship, and opportunities, STEM Without Barriers works to create more doorways into STEM for students everywhere.

💡

Every learner belongs in STEM.

We are building a community where curiosity is encouraged and every student has room to grow.

OUR STORY

From a small idea to a growing community.

STEM Without Barriers began as a student-led initiative — a small group of peers who noticed that the students who most needed STEM opportunities were often the ones with the fewest doorways in.

We started with free resources, grew into workshops and mentorship, and now connect learners with the people, programs, and possibilities that help them see themselves in STEM.

What started local has become a remote community serving students everywhere, built on a simple belief: every student deserves the chance to explore and thrive.
